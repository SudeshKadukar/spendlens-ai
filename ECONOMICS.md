# ECONOMICS

## Lead Value
If one customer buys $20,000/year in credits and a partner like Credex earns a 15% margin, one qualified lead is worth approximately $3,000 in gross profit.

## CAC Estimate
Estimated CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) via organic channels: $10 - $50.

## Conversion Funnels
- **Audit Completion to Lead Capture**: 20%
- **Lead Capture to Consultation Booking**: 10%
- **Consultation to Purchase**: 25%

## Scaling to $1M ARR
To reach $1M ARR in 18 months, we need approximately 334 active customers generating $3,000/year in margin each.
- Total Audits Needed: ~6,700
- Monthly Audits: ~372
