# GTM (Go-To-Market) Strategy

## Target User Persona: The Overwhelmed AI Leader
Our primary target user is the **Founding Engineer or Engineering Manager (EM)** at a high-growth AI startup (typically between the Seed and Series B stages). These companies usually employ between 5 and 75 people and are currently burning significant capital across a fragmented stack of AI tools. 

The core pain point we solve is "Spend Blindness." In high-velocity environments, developers often sign up for multiple experimental tools (e.g., Cursor for one project, GitHub Copilot for another, Claude for research, and ChatGPT for general tasks) and then forget to cancel old subscriptions or manage seats as the team scales up or down. The Engineering Manager is usually the one who eventually sees the $4,500/month tool bill and realizes they have no centralized visibility into who is using what. SpendLens AI gives them the data they need to justify a consolidation plan to their CTO or CFO.

## Online Presence & Digital Hangouts
We will establish a presence where AI-native founders and engineers already congregate to discuss their "AI Stack":
- **X (Twitter)**: We will engage deeply with the "Build in Public" and "AI Engineering" bubbles. We won't just post links; we will publish "AI Spend Benchmark" threads (e.g., "We audited 50 Series A startups—here's the median spend on coding agents"). This data-driven approach positions us as an authority and drives high-intent referral traffic.
- **Reddit**: We will target r/SaaS, r/startups, and r/webdev with "Audit Case Studies." These posts will detail how a specific (anonymized) 20-person team saved $1,200/month by consolidating their licenses and moving to discounted API credit pools.
- **Discord/Slack Communities**: We will participate in high-signal communities like "Vercel & Friends," "Latent Space," and "Founder Collective." Our goal is to be the go-to recommendation when a founder asks, "How can I cut my OpenAI bill?"
- **Hacker News**: We will publish deep-dive technical articles on "The Hidden Economics of AI APIs in 2026," targeting the front page with educational content that subtly leads into our audit tool.

## Zero-Budget Acquisition: The First 100 Users
1. **The "Check My Stack" Viral Loop**: On our results page, we provide a "Share My Audit" button. This generates a custom, visually appealing Open Graph image that highlights the percentage of savings discovered and the logos of the tools audited. Founders often use these "Efficiency Wins" as a form of social proof to show investors they are running a lean, smart operation. This creates a powerful referral loop within founder circles.
2. **Direct "Audit" Outreach**: We will scrape lists of recently funded startups from platforms like Y Combinator's "Work at a Startup" or Product Hunt. We will reach out directly to their CTOs or EMs with a low-pressure, high-value message: "Hey, saw you're scaling your AI team. We built a free tool to check if you're overpaying for your developer stack. It takes 60 seconds and usually finds $200-$1,000 in monthly waste."
3. **Product Hunt Launch**: We will launch SpendLens AI as a free community tool. Our goal isn't just "Product of the Day" status; it's to capture the initial wave of "early adopter" emails and feedback to refine our audit rules.

## Unfair Distribution Channel: The Credex Partnership
Our primary unfair advantage is our tight integration with **Credex**. By functioning as a high-value "lead magnet" for Credex, we can provide a level of auditing and specific savings recommendations that would typically cost thousands of dollars. Credex provides us with the latest insider knowledge on enterprise credit pricing, which we bake into our deterministic engine. This allows us to provide "Insider Recommendations" (like how to get 15% off Anthropic credits) that our competitors simply don't have access to.

## Week-1 Traction Goal: Validating the Pain Point
- **75 Completed Audits**: We want to see a consistent volume of users finishing the entire form to prove that the "effort-to-value" ratio is correct.
- **15 Qualified Leads**: We are looking for users who provide a valid work email and show more than $300/month in potential savings.
- **5 Partner Handoffs**: Our ultimate goal for Week 1 is to see 5 high-value users click the "Get a free consultation with Credex" button, validating that they are ready to take action on the savings we've discovered.
