# GTM (Go-To-Market) Strategy

## Target User
Founder or engineering manager at a 5–50 person AI startup spending $300–$5,000/month on AI tools.

## Online Presence
- **Twitter/X**: AI engineering circles, "Build in Public" community.
- **Reddit**: r/startups, r/SaaS, r/webdev.
- **Product Hunt**: For initial launch.
- **LinkedIn**: Targeting CTOs and EMs.

## Zero-Budget Acquisition (First 100 Users)
1. **Direct Outreach**: Message founders on Twitter/LinkedIn who post about their AI stack.
2. **Community Engagement**: Share helpful audit insights in relevant subreddits and Discord servers.
3. **Viral Loops**: Encourage users to share their audit results (anonymized) on social media.

## Unfair Distribution Channel
Partnerships with AI developer tools or credit providers (like Credex) to offer audits as a value-add.

## Week-1 Traction Goal
Reach 50 completed audits and capture 10 leads.
