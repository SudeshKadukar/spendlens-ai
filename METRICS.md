# METRICS

## North Star Metric
Qualified savings opportunities discovered per week.

## Input Metrics
- **Audit Completion Rate**: Percentage of users who start and finish the audit.
- **Email Capture Rate**: Percentage of users who provide their email after the audit.
- **Consultation Booking Rate**: Percentage of high-savings users who book a Credex consultation.

## Pivot Trigger
If fewer than 5% of completed audits show more than $500/month savings after 100 audits, change targeting or audit rules.
