# METRICS

## North Star Metric: Qualified Savings Opportunities Discovered per Week
Our North Star Metric is the **Total Dollar Amount of Qualified Monthly Savings Discovered per Week**. Unlike vanity metrics such as "Page Views" or even "User Signups," this metric directly measures the primary value proposition of SpendLens AI: identifying financial waste in the AI stack. It aligns our product development with our business goals. For example, if we discover $50,000 in potential monthly savings in a single week, we have effectively identified $600,000 in annual lead value for our partners. This metric forces us to focus on attracting high-intent users (CTOs and EMs at funded startups) rather than casual hobbyists who might only be spending $20/month.

## Input Metrics: Tracking the Value Funnel
To move the North Star, we track a strictly defined conversion funnel that measures the efficiency of our user experience and the quality of our traffic:

1. **Audit Completion Rate (Target: >40%)**: This is the percentage of visitors who successfully submit the spend form. Since our form requires manual entry, this is our biggest friction point. We monitor this to ensure our UI remains streamlined and that the "How it Works" section effectively builds enough anticipation to overcome the effort of data entry.
2. **Email Capture Rate (Target: >25%)**: This is the percentage of users who provide their work email after seeing their results. This is our ultimate "Trust Metric." If users see their savings but don't want to save the report, it means either the savings weren't significant enough or our results didn't feel credible.
3. **High-Savings Concentration (Target: >15%)**: This tracks the percentage of audits that reveal >$500/month in potential savings. This is a proxy for our marketing quality. If this number drops, it means our distribution channels are attracting users who don't have the "problem" we solve (i.e., they aren't spending enough to care).
4. **Consultation Booking Rate (Target: >10% of High-Savings Users)**: This measures the effectiveness of our Credex CTA. It tells us if our "handoff" from audit to actual savings capture is compelling enough for a busy founder to book a call.

## Pivot Trigger: The 100-Audit Reality Check
We have established a strict **Pivot Trigger** based on the **High-Savings Concentration** metric to ensure we don't spend months building a product that no one needs or that doesn't generate high-value leads.

**The Pivot Condition:** If, after our first 100 completed audits, fewer than 5% of them show potential savings of >$500/month, we will immediately halt current feature development and trigger a strategic pivot. A low concentration indicates that the "AI Tool Overspend" problem might be smaller or more niche than our initial research suggested.

**Potential Pivot Paths:**
- **The "Enterprise" Pivot**: Shift our targeting to Series C+ companies where the spend is in the $100k+ range, requiring a move from a self-service tool to a "concierge" auditing service.
- **The "Governance" Pivot**: If savings aren't the pain point, move toward "AI Security & Compliance," helping companies track which "Shadow AI" tools their employees are using to prevent data leakage.
- **The "Efficiency" Pivot**: Instead of focusing on "Costs," focus on "ROI"—helping teams measure the actual productivity gain of their AI tools to justify the spend to their CFO.
